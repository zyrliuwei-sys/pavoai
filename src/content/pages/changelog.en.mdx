## Video generation

Seedance 2.0 and Wan 2.7 create five-second videos from text, with 720p and 1080p options. Seedance Fast is available at 720p.

## Image generation

Nano Banana 2 and Seedream 4 create images from text, with 1K, 2K and 4K quality options.

## Creations and downloads

Results generated with a registered account are saved in [My Creations](/settings/works). Guests can leave an email to receive a download link. Without an email, the download is available only on the current page and is not restored after a refresh.

## Billing

Generation uses purchased credits. See [Pricing](/pricing) for credit packs and subscriptions.
