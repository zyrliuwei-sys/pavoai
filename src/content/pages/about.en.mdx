## From a prompt to a first take

Pavo AI brings text-to-video and text-to-image generation into one browser workspace. Describe the scene, choose a model, aspect ratio and quality, then generate and download the result.

## Available today

- **Video:** Seedance 2.0 and Wan 2.7, with five-second clips at 720p or 1080p.
- **Images:** Nano Banana 2 and Seedream 4, with model-specific quality options.
- **Billing:** monthly plans, yearly plans and one-time credit packs. Review the [pricing page](/pricing) for current prices and generation costs.

The model menu in the generator lists the available models. The gallery provides visual inspiration and does not represent results from the connected models.
