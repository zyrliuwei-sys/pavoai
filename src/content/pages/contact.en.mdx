## Email support

Email [support@pavoai.net](mailto:support@pavoai.net) for account help, billing questions, privacy requests or product feedback.

## Send a support ticket

If you have an account, [open Support tickets](/settings/tickets) to send a request and read replies. You can also use the support button on the homepage or in your account. If tickets are unavailable, use the email address above.

## Help us investigate

Include the page where the issue occurred, what you expected and what happened. For billing questions, include your order number; for generation issues, include the model and approximate time. Screenshots are helpful. Never send your password, API keys or full payment card details.
