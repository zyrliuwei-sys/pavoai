## Open the browser workspace

The Pavo AI workspace for this website is [pavoai.net](/). You can generate images and short videos directly in a desktop or mobile browser.

## Choose the output

The Video tab includes Seedance 2.0 and Wan 2.7. The Image tab includes Nano Banana 2 and Seedream 4. Choose a model, aspect ratio and quality, write a prompt and review the credit cost.

## Find registered creations

Sign in before generating to keep your results in [My Creations](/settings/works). The page lists the prompt, model, date and task status, with preview and download actions when a result is ready.

## Guest delivery

Registration is optional for checkout and generation. Leave an email when available to receive a download link. Without an email, download the result on the current page. A refresh or closed page does not provide a way to restore that guest result.

For account or payment help, visit [Contact](/contact).
