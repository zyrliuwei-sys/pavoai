## Review your first clip on the phone

Try a prompt that identifies the subject, the movement and what stays fixed. “The paper lantern sways once, gently, while the cord stays attached. The camera remains fixed and the lantern stays fully visible.” Check the clip with sound off so you can focus on the motion. Review the end frame, then play the clip once in the orientation you intend to publish.

If the edges warp, try a smaller sway or a reference with more room around the subject. If the effect looks fine but the clip is hard to frame vertically, try a taller source image. Change one thing between runs and keep the original photo so you can see what helped.
