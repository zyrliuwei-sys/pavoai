## Create on your phone

Open [pavoai.net](/) in your mobile browser. The same text-to-video and text-to-image generator is available on desktop and mobile. No app installation is required for this website.

## Set up a portrait result

Choose Video for a clip up to 15 seconds, or Image for a still. Select the duration before generating. Select one of the connected models and choose 9:16 for a portrait composition. Describe the subject and leave space around it if you plan to add text later in another tool.

## Keep track of downloads

Sign in to save results in [My Creations](/settings/works), where you can return to preview and download them. Your browser may place downloaded media in its Downloads folder rather than the photo library; use the device's share or save controls to move the file if needed.

## Generating as a guest

An optional email lets you receive a result link when the work is ready. Without an email, download before refreshing or closing the page. If an automatic download does not start, tap Download on the result card.

Review the credit cost before generating. Repeating a generation creates a new paid task.
