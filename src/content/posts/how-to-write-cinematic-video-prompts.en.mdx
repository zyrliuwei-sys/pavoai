## Give one shot a clear brief

A five-second clip is a small unit of a larger story. Describe one subject, a setting and one visible action. Avoid asking the model to show several scenes or a full plot in one request.

## Camera, light and motion

Specify a camera movement such as a slow push-in or a fixed medium shot. Describe the light, then the subject's movement. For example:

> A chef plating a dessert, close-up with shallow depth of field, warm side light, one hand gently placing a berry, camera held still.

## Change one thing at a time

Review the result and decide what needs changing: the framing, the movement or the light. Keep the rest of the prompt stable when trying again. Each new generation consumes credits.
