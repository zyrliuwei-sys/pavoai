## What can you create with Pavo AI?

The current text-to-video workspace offers:

- **Two video models:** Seedance 2.0 and Wan 2.7.
- **Duration:** 5–15 seconds, selected in whole seconds.
- **Resolution:** 720p or 1080p. Seedance Fast is available at 720p.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1.
- **Input:** a written text prompt.

Use it to explore a product scene, a portrait-format social clip or a cinematic shot. You can download your result and continue working in your preferred editing tool. The current workflow provides generation and downloads, without a timeline editor or uploaded-image animation.

## Create your first video in six steps

### 1. Open Video mode

Go to the [generator](/?mode=video#generator) and select **Video**. Sign in before generating if you want to revisit the result in My Creations.

### 2. Choose a model

Select **Seedance 2.0** or **Wan 2.7**. Both offer the duration and resolution options listed above. Review the displayed credit cost when switching models or settings.

For a comparison of the available controls and costs, read the [video model guide](/blog/veo-3-1-vs-kling-3-0).

### 3. Set the duration and format

For a first attempt, try a five-second scene with one clear movement. Choose **9:16** for a portrait composition, **16:9** for a wide shot or **1:1** for a square frame.

Set the duration in the controls. Writing “a 15-second video” in your prompt does not change a five-second duration setting.

### 4. Describe one shot

Include the subject, setting, action, camera and lighting. Keep the description focused enough to fit the selected duration.

> A clear glass perfume bottle on pale limestone. The camera slowly pushes closer. Soft window light creates gentle reflections on the glass. A quiet studio background, realistic materials, one continuous shot.

### 5. Check the cost and generate

Confirm the model, duration, resolution and credit quote before submitting. A new generation uses credits, so review your settings before trying another version. Processing time depends on the selected model and its queue.

### 6. Preview and download

Watch the full clip. Check the subject’s shape, movement and background before downloading or using it in a larger project.

## Three prompts to try

These are suggested starting points to adapt, rather than documented generation results. The cover image is an illustration for the guide. For your own tests, keep the prompt, settings, number of attempts and credit cost alongside each downloaded clip.

### A product close-up

Suggested starting settings: **5 seconds · 720p · 16:9**. Choose either connected video model and check the quote before submitting.

> A matte white skincare bottle standing on warm beige stone, surrounded by a clean studio background. A slow camera push-in reveals the surface texture. Soft light from the left, subtle shadows, realistic materials, one continuous shot.

Look for a stable bottle shape, consistent reflections and a camera move that feels gradual. This prompt describes a product concept from text; it does not preserve an uploaded product photograph or a specific brand’s packaging.

### A vertical coffee scene

Suggested starting settings: **5 seconds · 720p · 9:16**.

> A sage green ceramic cup filled with hot coffee on a wooden café table. A thin curl of steam rises gently. Soft morning light enters from the side. Fixed camera, shallow depth of field, calm atmosphere, one continuous shot.

Keep the cup comfortably inside the frame. If you plan to add a caption later, describe space above the subject and add the text in your editing tool.

### A cinematic city shot

Suggested starting settings: **5 seconds · 720p · 16:9**.

> A single red tram moves slowly from left to right through a rain-soaked city street at dusk. Warm windows reflect on the wet pavement. Locked camera, blue evening light, realistic motion, one uninterrupted shot.

Review whether the tram keeps its shape and whether the surrounding buildings remain stable as it moves. To compare models, use the same prompt, duration, resolution and aspect ratio for both, then review each output.

## How to improve a result

Start with the part that needs the most attention:

- **A crowded scene:** reduce the number of subjects and actions.
- **Chaotic motion:** choose a fixed camera and one movement.
- **An unclear composition:** specify the framing, such as “a close-up of the bottle” or “a wide shot of the street.”
- **An unstable subject:** simplify the action and review the result before adding more detail.

Keep a copy of your previous prompt and settings. Changing one element per attempt makes the differences easier to assess. Results vary between runs; a prompt describes your intended shot without guaranteeing a particular output.

For more examples of camera and lighting instructions, read [How to Write Video Prompts That Look Cinematic](/blog/how-to-write-cinematic-video-prompts).

## Questions before you start

### Is Pavo AI video generation free?

Generation uses purchased credits. Review the displayed cost before submitting a task and check the [available plans](/pricing).

### Can I upload a photo and animate it?

The current generator at pavoai.net accepts text prompts. Uploaded-image animation is not available in this workflow. The separate Image mode generates still images from text.

### Can I create a longer finished video?

Individual generated clips support up to 15 seconds. You can download separate clips and assemble a longer sequence in another editor.

### What happens if generation fails?

Credits for tasks confirmed as failed by the provider are returned automatically. A successful generation that you choose not to use still consumes credits. For a task or payment issue, contact [support](/contact) with the task details.

### Can I use a video in a commercial project?

Review the [content rights and usage terms](/terms-of-service#content-rights) and any applicable model provider terms for your planned use. Check generated content for rights and suitability before publishing it.
