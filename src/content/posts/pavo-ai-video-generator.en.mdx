## Start with one scene

Open the [video generator](/?mode=video#generator). Choose Seedance 2.0 or Wan 2.7, select 720p or 1080p and set the aspect ratio. Choose a duration from 5 to 15 seconds for either model. Credits update with the selected duration; a longer duration in your prompt does not override this setting.

## Write a focused prompt

Name the subject, setting, light and one movement. A short clip works best with a simple action rather than a full script.

> A silver watch on dark stone. A slow camera push-in, a narrow strip of light across the face, soft reflections and a still background.

## Review the cost

Generation requires purchased credits. The generator displays the cost for your selected model and quality. Credit packs and subscription options are listed on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Download and continue

Preview the result before using it. You can download the clip and edit it in your preferred editor. Pavo AI currently provides generation and downloads; it does not include a timeline editor or uploaded-image animation.

Results generated while signed in to a registered account are saved in [My Creations](/settings/works). Guests can receive an email download link, or download on the current page without leaving an email. Refreshing does not restore a guest result.
