## Two connected video models

Pavo AI currently offers **Seedance 2.0** and **Wan 2.7** for text-to-video. Both support 5–15 seconds, with 720p and 1080p options. Select the duration before generating. The model menu shows the available routes; each request generates one clip.

## Compare the cost before generating

For a five-second video, Wan 2.7 uses 3,200 credits at 720p or 4,800 at 1080p. Seedance 2.0 uses 7,960 credits at 720p or 19,880 at 1080p. Seedance Fast at 720p uses 6,440 credits. The amount displayed in the generator is the cost of the selected settings.

Start with one clear prompt and a lower-cost option when exploring an idea. A second generation is a separate paid task, even if you reuse the prompt.

## Give each model the same brief

Describe a subject, a setting, a camera movement and lighting. For example:

> A small red sailboat on a calm lake at dawn. Slow sideways camera movement, soft mist and warm light. Keep the boat fully in frame.

Results vary between runs. Compare motion, framing and unwanted changes before deciding which result suits your project. No specific speed or visual quality is guaranteed.

## Keep the result

Registered users can find their generated clips in [My Creations](/settings/works). Guests may leave an email to receive a download link. Without an email, download from the current page before refreshing.
