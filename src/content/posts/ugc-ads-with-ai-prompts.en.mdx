UGC ads work because they do not look like ads — a creator-style close-up, messy apartment light, honest tone. AI video now produces that look from one product photo, and the winners follow a pattern: hook in the first second, product in hand by the second, one claim per clip. Here are ten prompts built on that pattern. Replace the bracketed parts and go.

## The three rules first

1. **One claim per clip.** Ten seconds, one benefit. Cut everything else.
2. **Imperfection sells.** "Handheld, slightly shaky, natural window light" beats "professional studio" every time in UGC.
3. **Product in frame early.** The viewer should know what the ad is for by second two.

## The 10 prompts

1. **Unboxing hook:** `Handheld selfie-style video, a young woman opening a [product] package at her kitchen table, natural morning window light, genuine surprised smile, slightly shaky camera.`
2. **Before/after:** `Handheld phone video, split moment: cluttered desk, then a quick pan to the same desk organized with [product], warm home lighting, satisfied nod to camera.`
3. **Testimonial:** `Sitting-on-bedroom-floor UGC angle, a man holding [product] up to the camera, soft lamp light, talking directly to the viewer with an easy grin, casual tone.`
4. **Problem/solution:** `Handheld close-up, a frustrated commuter juggling bags in the rain, hard cut to them smiling using [product], muted city colors, realistic motion.`
5. **Day-in-life:** `Casual vlog-style clip, morning routine on a bathroom counter, [product] in frame and in use, golden-hour light through a window, calm slow motion.`
6. **POV demo:** `First-person POV hands opening [product] and using it on a wooden desk, natural daylight, shallow depth of field, steady confident motion.`
7. **Reaction shot:** `Vertical handheld clip, a woman trying [product] for the first time and reacting with a laugh, slightly overexposed indoor light, authentic energy.`
8. **Comparison:** `Handheld pan across three rival products on a shelf, settling on [product], cool supermarket light, deliberate slow camera move.`
9. **ASMR detail:** `Extreme close-up, macro lens slowly gliding over the texture and packaging of [product], soft studio key light, crisp slow motion.`
10. **CTA closer:** `Direct-to-camera UGC shot, a creator holding [product] beside their face, pointing at an off-screen link, warm ring-light glow, friendly persuasive tone.`

## Turning ten prompts into an ad

Sequence 1, 3 and 10 and you have a classic 30-second UGC structure: hook, trust, close. Keep the light and camera language identical across the three so they cut together like one creator's footage — that consistency trick is worth more than any single beautiful shot.

Each prompt is a starting point. Run it, keep the take where the motion feels most accidental, and rewrite only the bracketed parts for your next product. That loop — photo in, ad out — is what Ad Studio on Pavo AI automates, but the prompts above work in any studio.
