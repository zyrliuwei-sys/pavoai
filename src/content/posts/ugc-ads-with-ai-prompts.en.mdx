## Describe the product in words

The current Pavo AI video generator takes text, not a product image. Describe the product's shape, material, color and surroundings. Generated details may differ from the real product, so review the output carefully before using it.

## Three ideas to try

**Studio light:** A clear perfume bottle on black stone, a soft reflection beneath it, one narrow light moving across the glass, fixed camera.

**Everyday setting:** A ceramic coffee cup beside an open book, morning window light, steam rising gently, slow push-in.

**Outdoor texture:** A plain red water bottle on a hiking rock, green forest in the background, sunlight through leaves, subtle sideways camera movement.

## Choose settings and review

Use Seedance 2.0 or Wan 2.7 for a five-second clip. Select the aspect ratio and 720p or 1080p quality, then review the credit cost. These prompts are starting points, not tested conversion claims.

Download the result to add your own verified product details, branding and captions in another editing tool. Registered users can return to [My Creations](/settings/works) to find their clips.
